=== ABOUT RCBA ===
The Rotaract Club of Bombay Airport (RCBA) is a community-based non-profit organisation
under the Rotaract District 3141. It is based in Mumbai, India.
Motto: "Act For Impact"
The club is made up of agile young people who believe in uplifting society through
social and community service, development of individuals, and recreation.
Website: https://www.rcbombayairport.org

=== OUR PROJECTS ===

--- Community Service ---
1. Kranti
   An initiative that promotes menstruation health and cleanliness while empowering
   women and girls to take better care of themselves.

2. Open School 6.0
   Free education provided to over 310 kids from four vernacular medium schools,
   driven by 60+ dedicated volunteers.

3. Happy Rays 2.0
   Delivers the joy of a dream birthday celebration to every child regardless of
   socioeconomic status.

4. Toys for Tots 3.0
   Over 1,000 toys donated to underprivileged children, embodying the true spirit
   of Christmas and kindness.

5. Gul Daastan
   Stories brought to life to help kids learn life skills and good habits in a fun,
   loving way.

6. Project Sahara
   Creates a caring community that respects and cares for its elderly citizens.

7. Jashn-E-Rang
   Spreads joy, creativity, and inclusivity through colors and artistic expression.

8. Share Your Closet 4.0
   Over 1,200 bags with 7,000+ apparel items donated to underprivileged people in
   Mumbai during Diwali.

--- Club Service & Sports ---
9. Dholida 5.0
   Navratri celebration through garba and dandiya with 200+ members, celebrating
   India's rich heritage.

10. Prom 4.0
    An enchanting night of sparkling lights, vibrant streamers — Prom 2025!

11. Camping 2.0
    A beach camp celebrating the year's accomplishments while enjoying coastal wonders.

12. Midnight Cycling
    A thrilling night ride through the streets of Mumbai for cycling enthusiasts
    and adventure seekers.

13. Potluck Palooza
    Fosters camaraderie and bonds among RCBA members through a shared culinary experience.

14. Mehfil - an Open Mic
    A stage for in-house and external artists to showcase literary, musical, and
    performative talents.

15. Heart Beats
    An event focused on open conversations, dance-offs, and deep connections among members.

--- Professional Development ---
16. Empowering Voices
    Helps participants step out of their comfort zones and develop public speaking
    and communication skills.

17. Khelkood 2.0
    Brings members together for a nostalgic experience by revisiting childhood games.

18. FIKA 2.0
    Focused on building connections and forming new friendships in a warm atmosphere.

--- Supporting Avenue Projects ---
19. Capture'O'graphy
    A year-long photography initiative to appreciate iconic Aamchi Mumbai.

20. Afterthoughts
    A book club that unites friends and knowledge for great reads and engaging company.

21. Glam & Connect
    An exclusive, creative, interactive experience crafted specifically for young women.
